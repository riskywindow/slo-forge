# Adversarial architecture and performance-methodology review

Review date: 2026-08-01 (America/Los_Angeles)

This document records the initial adversarial review and the disposition of every finding after the final hardening run. The release evidence is the post-review run in `artifacts/demo` and `reports/demo`, generated from source revision `2c889e6956ac73a1a530f1abb1d7407f70219ffe`. Pre-hardening artifact values were deliberately removed so they cannot be mistaken for current results. Detailed implementation evidence is in the root `FINAL_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.md`.

## Final verdict

SLOForge has a substantive compiler and runtime core: a strict cross-language IR, explicit feasibility/profile/model/selection passes, deterministic Rust simulation, a bounded streaming gateway, rejected-candidate diagnostics, safe controller evaluation, and two reconciled artifact hash chains. It is materially more than a YAML generator.

No high-severity issue remains in the exercised CPU path. The final review identified and fixed six high-severity defects before the immutable evidence run. The remaining boundaries are medium severity and are stated in the public limitations: generated GPU deployment images are an operator contract, the controller actuates a Rust twin rather than a live fleet manager, singleton benchmark summaries are not multi-run confidence intervals, OpenTelemetry output is archival rather than collector-exported, and GPU/cloud paths were not exercised on this host.

| M-01 | Controller results were generated by an analytical self-model. | Improved. Predictive and reactive actions now execute on separate, identically seeded Rust digital twins with independently retained scenarios and raw traces. Live fleet actuation remains out of scope for this host run. |
| M-02 | Diagnosis used configured duration as latency and had no negative windows. | Fixed. ## Final evidence audit

- The selected configuration is `cfg-aad9cd4cfa41`: three `mock-fast` replicas, concurrency six, batch limit 2048, chunked prefill, two warm replicas, and SLO-slack routing.
- The optimizer serialized 540 candidate evaluations, 27 uncertainty-adjusted feasible candidates, three direct measured anchors, and a four-point Pareto frontier.
- The compiled plan predicts 192.349 ms p95 TTFT, 3.471 ms p99 ITL, and 2.477182 USD per million tokens. The respective TTFT and ITL uncertainty radii are retained in the plan; selection uses their conservative margins.
- Held-out prefill MAPE is 8.401% with 91.667% interval coverage; held-out decode MAPE is 1.009% with 100% coverage.
- The live compiled gateway topology completed 120/120 streamed requests. The separately faulted simulator replay attained 97.5% of request deadlines and intentionally records three missed deadlines rather than hiding the negative result.
- The predictive Rust-twin controller had zero deadline misses at 0.036939 USD versus two misses at 0.035481 USD for the reactive baseline. This is a one-seed digital-twin comparison, not a claim of live-fleet superiority.
- Diagnosis classified all eight injected fault classes, produced no false positive in eight negative windows, and spent a measured mean 0.005666 ms in the rule classifier on this host. This is closed-set evidence only.
- The report command verified all 27 indexed artifact digests and reconciled all 24 `EvidenceBundle.artifact_hashes` before generating Markdown, HTML, Prometheus, OpenTelemetry-shaped JSON, Chrome trace, and SVG outputs.

## Compiler and backend assessment

The compiler claim is justified by the typed/versioned IR, migrations, feasibility diagnostics, calibrated service models, constrained optimization, non-dominated frontier, deployment lowering, and hash-linked evidence. Local and source-Docker paths are executed. Kubernetes, Modal, and Truss outputs are offline validated against current pinned tooling and schemas; no claim is made that they were deployed. GPU engine images and credentials remain operator inputs, and no paid resource was created.

## Claims intentionally not made

- H1 and H2 are not established without real, fresh GPU/engine trials.
- The mock-backend CPU metrics do not generalize to Qwen3 or unseen hardware.
- A one-seed predictive-controller result is not production autoscaler validation.
- Closed-set fault diagnosis is not an estimate of open-world incident accuracy.
- Static Modal/Truss validation is not a cloud deployment smoke test.

These limitations are first-class evidence outcomes, not omitted work or substituted measurements.
